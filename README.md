# The datasets "Homestead_2019_Load" and "QLD_2019_Load" are derived from national electricity demand data provided by the Australian Energy Market Operator and the City of Homestead (Florida, USA) Public Services Energy Department, respectively. They include load data for Queensland, Australia (QLD2019) for the entire year of 2019, and for the city of Homestead, USA, covering the two-year period from 2018 to 2019. The sampling intervals for these datasets are 30 minutes and 1 hour, respectively, with each dataset containing 17,520 data points.

# QuanZhou_1h dataset consists of power grid load data for Quanzhou City, Fujian Province, China, covering the period from 2016 to 2018 [81]. The original data were sampled at 15-minute intervals; these were resampled to a 1-hour interval, resulting in a total of 26,304 data points.（Engineering Applications）
